About me

I'm Ángel — a software engineering student at UANL (FIME) who treats interfaces the way an architect treats a room: every measurement considered, every material chosen on purpose.

Since May 2026 I've been a professional intern at IDN Ingeniería y Soluciones Industriales, where I test legacy .NET systems, administer SQL Server, configure virtual machines and networks, and write the manuals and video tutorials that keep industrial software usable. Beyond work, I build iOS apps with Swift and SwiftUI and take part in the UANL iOS Development Lab.

I enjoy taking apart complicated problems until only the essential shape remains, then building systems that carry real weight for the people who use them.
